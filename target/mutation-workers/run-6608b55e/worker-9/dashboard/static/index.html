Swarm detector

Finds groups of posts that are both similar in text and synchronised in time, and the accounts behind them: the footprint of brigading, astroturfing and bot networks. It flags patterns for a person to review; it does not prove who is behind them.

1Choose a dataset

A CSV, TSV, JSON or JSON Lines file with one post per row. It needs a content column and a time-posted column. An account column is optional and unlocks account types.

Preview first rows

2Add sample posts (optional)

Paste posts you already find suspicious. The tool looks for posts like them and checks whether they were posted in sync. Leave empty to scan the whole dataset.

3Run the detection

Lower finds looser matches and more false alarms.
