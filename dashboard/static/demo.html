Sargas

Sargas spots bot-nets by looking at groups of signups, stopping the fake accounts behind propaganda and free-trial abuse. The demo is three live sites: one being protected, the detector's dashboard, and a console for attacking it.

1 The site being protected botswarm.tech

Neighbr, a satirical "Tinder for horses" with a free tier. Sign up, ideally on your phone, and you'll see your own live bot score with the reasons behind it.

 2 The Sargas dashboard guard.botswarm.tech

Every signup's score and reasons, bot groups as they're detected (with AI summaries), the review queue, IP bans, and how Sargas did on labelled test data.

 3 The attack console attack.botswarm.tech

Launch bot campaigns against Neighbr, from a simple script to a bot-net with realistic names and a new IP for every account, then watch them get caught on the dashboard. Needs the demo key, which we'll give you at our table or on request.

Best order: open the dashboard, sign up on Neighbr from your phone, then launch an attack and watch the dashboard.
